We only get paid when you do.

Silver Bullet is a performance-based marketing firm. No retainers, no hourly billing, no invoice for a deck. We take a share of the revenue we generate — which means if it doesn't work, we don't get paid.

How the arrangement works

Three steps, no surprises. If we can't find enough upside to make the deal worth doing, we'll tell you that in the first conversation.

01

We audit what you have

Your numbers, your funnel, your margins. We find where money is leaking and what it would take to fix it.

02

We agree on the split

A share of revenue or profit above an agreed baseline. Written down, capped, and reviewed on a schedule.

03

We go build

Campaigns, pages, creative, tracking. You see the same dashboard we do, and our cut is calculated off it.
