How this was measured

Counted on matters opened where the enquiry is attributable to a channel we ran, and recognised on fees billed rather than matters signed.

Situation

A full pipeline of the wrong work. High volume, low value, and partners spending their days on consultations that were never going to be worth taking. More leads would only have made the problem worse.

Strategy

We made the intake harder rather than easier. A qualification step before anyone could book, targeting shifted onto the matter types that carry real fees, and a follow-up sequence for the enquiries worth a second attempt. Enquiry volume fell by about a third.

Results

Annual revenue moved from $5.5M to $7.9M across 10 months, an increase of +$2.4M. We were paid a share of that increase and nothing else — no retainer, and no fee for the months before it moved.

“Our enquiries went down and our revenue went up. I have stopped trying to explain that to other firms.”

Ellis Calder, senior partner, Calder & Roe
