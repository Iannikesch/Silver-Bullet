# Silver Bullet Agency site: feedback from 9/16 call with Ethan

Context for Claude: This is every website-related point from a recorded call where Ethan (agency owner) reviewed the current site and compared it to competitor sites. Read DESIGN.md, tokens.css, and CLAUDE.md first. Keep the site vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Deadline: ASAP (tonight).

## Overall direction
- Should feel like a big-time, established marketing agency. More "in your face." Ethan likes the content but wants more impact.
- Everything feels too small. Scale up type, logos, and sections.
- Layout is too centered/narrow. Content should fill the page width the way the reference sites do.
- Too rigid: boxes feel rigid, nothing feels organic. Add texture and softer, more organic surfaces and motion.
- Remove ALL em dashes from site copy.
- Check and refine mobile.

## Hero / bullet interaction
- Ethan does NOT like the click-and-hold behavior on the bullet. It feels rigid.
- The bullet should travel across the screen smoothly on its own, not only on first load or when held.

## Remove / replace
- Remove the shaking scroll graphic with the click button (the scroll-down cue section). Motion/interactivity is fine, but replace it with a different graphic that actually makes sense.
- (Note: an auto-scroll-slow version was supposed to be merged and wasn't. Fix or replace.)

## "Proficient in" tools section
- Rename "Proficient in" to "Partnered with".
- Add more platforms/CRMs/tools: HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, and similar.
- Add about two more rows/columns of platform logos.
- Ethan likes logo rows scrolling in opposite directions. Keep/expand that.

## Client logos / photos
- Make client logos bigger and more visible.
- Ethan will send corporate logos of companies he has worked with. Leave room for them.
- Some photos will get swapped out later.
- Legal constraint: do not present clients as case studies without approval. Use language like "Our team has worked with" instead.

## Industries
- Consolidate to top 3: Ecom, Home Services (HVAC, plumbing, moving & storage), B2B. CPG was also mentioned as an option.

## Capabilities
- Restructure: split into B2B and B2C (or "Agencies" and "Brands"), with capabilities listed underneath each.
- Nav "Capabilities" dropdown should follow the same structure.

## Lower section
- The section near the bottom (currently "fine") should be changed to show return on investment / results.

## Inline form
- Keep an inline form, but restyle it after the reference (Sliced Bread agency): systemized, multi-step feel. It must be a true inline form, not a button that opens a popup.
- Form submissions go to contact@silverbulletagency.com via Resend (in progress).

## Content structure to prep
- Build tabs/pages ready for: Blog, and educational resources (e-books, etc.) beyond case studies. Content will be delivered later.
- Case studies: reference sites link each client logo to its case study. Build structure so logos can link to case studies once approved.

## Tracking
- Competitor tags basically every button in GA4. Add click tracking to all CTAs/buttons.

## Still pending (don't block on these)
- Legal pages (privacy, terms): waiting on legal.
- Social links in footer: none yet, wire up later.
- Calendly booking link for "Book a call."

## Reference / competitor sites Ethan showed
- **Sliced Bread** (slicedbread.agency): liked a lot. Audit > Build > Measure > Compound process section, logo wall with linked case studies ("over a thousand brands trust us"), case study pages, simple service breakdown, inline form style.
- **Unnamed competitor (~$1.3M/mo)**: Ethan says the site is NOT good, just has movement, but the structure is worth copying: industry verticals each with their own service lists (e.g., Ecom growth: paid ads, Amazon, lifecycle retention; Coaching/info products: content, funnel building, sales; Local business: automotive, family entertainment).
- **Arm Candy**: direct competitor, serves both agencies and brands (source of the Agencies/Brands split idea). Ethan disliked their "featured technology integrations" framing. Don't call tools "integrations."
- Also mentioned: Thrive Digital, Inbound, Webtonic.
